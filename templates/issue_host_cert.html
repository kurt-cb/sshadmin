About Host Certificates

What are they?

SSH host certificates verify that you're connecting to the right server, replacing TOFU (Trust On First Use).

Benefits:

  • MITM attack prevention
  • No host key verification prompts
  • Time-limited validity
  • Centralized management

Configuration:

@cert-authority * <CA_PUB_KEY>
